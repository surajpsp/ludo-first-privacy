Privacy Policy

Ludo First · Effective date: 16 November 2026

In short: Ludo First has no accounts and no analytics of its own. It shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads (section 4). Offline games stay on your phone, apart from the coins they earn for the weekly Ranks. When you play online, the game sends only what the match needs – a nickname, a random player ID, your moves and the emoji you pick – and deletes it when the match ends. The weekly Ranks show your nickname and the coins you won in matches that week; that entry is deleted 30 days after your last match.

1. Who we are

Ludo First (package ID com.silentdev.ludofirst) is a Ludo board game developed by Silent Dev ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles information when you play it on Android or iOS.

2. Offline play

Games against the computer and Pass & Play games run entirely on your device. The only thing sent afterwards is the number of coins you won, which counts toward your weekly Ranks points (see section 3). Pass & Play pays no coins, so nothing about it is sent. Ads (section 4) still load while you play if your phone is online.

3. Online play

Online play (Quick match and Play with friends) uses Google Firebase, a service run by Google, as the game server. When you start online play the app:

  • Creates an anonymous player ID. Firebase Anonymous Authentication gives your copy of the app a random ID so a match knows which seat is yours. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
  • Sends the match data. While a match is running, the online room holds only:
DataWhyHow long it is kept
The nickname you type (it can be anything; you do not need to use your real name)Shown to the other players in the roomUntil the match ends
Your random player ID and seat colourTo know whose turn it isUntil the match ends
Your dice rolls and moves, and the secret numbers used to make the dice fairSo every phone shows the same gameUntil the match ends
Whether you are connectedTo play for you if you drop out, and show you as offlineUntil the match ends
The last emoji or preset phrase you sentShown to the other playersUntil the match ends

The whole room is deleted when the match ends. A room that is left unfinished is deleted automatically within one day.

Weekly Ranks. After a match against the computer or online that pays coins, the app sends your nickname, your random player ID and your total coins won this week to a weekly league that other players can see. That is the only thing kept between matches: one line per player, overwritten each time, and deleted automatically 30 days after your last match. We keep no profiles, other statistics or match history.

Names other players see. Nicknames are checked against a list of offensive words before they are sent and again before they are shown; a refused name is replaced by a standard one such as "Player 4821". On Ranks you can hide any player on your phone or report a name to us by email.

There is no typed chat. Players can only send emoji and preset phrases from a fixed list.

Google processes this data for us under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Firebase may also record technical details such as your IP address as part of providing the service.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free game shows a small banner and offers optional videos that give you coins. Both come from Google AdMob, a Google advertising service. To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and sends to Google:

DataUsed for
Your IP address, from which Google may estimate your general locationAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
How you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video viewsAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and hang rateAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device identifiers: the advertising ID and app set ID on Android; on iPhone, the identifier for vendors, and the advertising identifier only if you allow trackingAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data goes to Google, encrypted in transit, and Google handles it under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it about you as a person; AdMob gives us only totals such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before any ad loads, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the app asks whether it may track you for ads. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not used.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's Google settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Videos for coins are always optional; nothing in the game requires watching one.

5. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your device: your nickname, your coins, level, dice styles, board themes and recent games, the players you hid on Ranks, and a random secret for each online room you joined (used to make the dice fair and to rejoin a match after a dropped connection). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

6. What we do not do

  • No sign-up, login or user accounts.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. The only outside services are Google Firebase (online play and Ranks) and Google AdMob (ads).
  • No access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The only permission prompt is the iPhone's "allow tracking" question for ads, which you can decline.
  • We do not sell your data. Apart from what Google AdMob collects to show ads (section 4), nothing is used for advertising.
  • No real-money gaming, prizes or purchases.

7. Sharing a room code or result

When you tap "Share code" or "Share result", your phone's own share menu opens. What you share, and with whom, is up to you; the app does not see it.

8. Children

Ludo First is meant for players aged 13 and over, and its ads are not set up for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information in an online match, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

Offline games send no game data apart from your weekly Ranks points; ads still load whenever your phone is online. Online match data and Ranks entries are deleted automatically, as described above. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you or to have your Ranks entry deleted. Data collected by Google AdMob is held by Google; use the choices in section 4 or Google's own privacy tools.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to handle information differently – for example if in-app purchases are added – we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
