Privacy Policy

Ludo First · Effective date: 12 November 2026

In short: Ludo First has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Offline games never leave your phone. When you play online, the game sends only what the match needs – a nickname, a random player ID, your moves and the emoji you pick – and deletes it when the match ends.

1. Who we are

Ludo First (package ID com.silentdev.ludofirst) is a Ludo board game developed by Silent Dev ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles information when you play it on Android or iOS.

2. Offline play

Games against the computer and Pass & Play games run entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Online play

Online play (Quick match and Play with friends) uses Google Firebase, a service run by Google, as the game server. When you start online play the app:

  • Creates an anonymous player ID. Firebase Anonymous Authentication gives your copy of the app a random ID so a match knows which seat is yours. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
  • Sends the match data. While a match is running, the online room holds only:
DataWhyHow long it is kept
The nickname you type (it can be anything; you do not need to use your real name)Shown to the other players in the roomUntil the match ends
Your random player ID and seat colourTo know whose turn it isUntil the match ends
Your dice rolls and moves, and the secret numbers used to make the dice fairSo every phone shows the same gameUntil the match ends
Whether you are connectedTo play for you if you drop out, and show you as offlineUntil the match ends
The last emoji or preset phrase you sentShown to the other playersUntil the match ends

The whole room is deleted when the match ends. A room that is left unfinished is deleted automatically within one day. We keep no profiles, statistics, match history or leaderboards.

There is no typed chat. Players can only send emoji and preset phrases from a fixed list.

Google processes this data for us under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Firebase may also record technical details such as your IP address as part of providing the service.

4. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your device: your online nickname, and a random secret for each online room you joined (used to make the dice fair and to rejoin a match after a dropped connection). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

5. What we do not do

  • No sign-up, login or user accounts.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The app asks for no special permissions.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • No real-money gaming, prizes or purchases.

6. Sharing a room code or result

When you tap "Share code" or "Share result", your phone's own share menu opens. What you share, and with whom, is up to you; the app does not see it.

7. Children

Ludo First is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has entered personal information in an online match, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

You can play every offline mode without sending any data. Online match data is deleted automatically, as described above. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted; because we keep no player records, there is usually nothing left once a match ends.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts to handle information differently – for example if ads or in-app purchases are added – we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
